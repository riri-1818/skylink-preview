About us

Broadening options for a brighter future.

Connecting people, ideas and things of value across borders. That is the work we began in Sydney in 2019.

Mission

People who want to study or work abroad. Companies and regions that want Australians to know what they offer.
What they share is not knowing how to take the first step.

We started with career support for international students. Rather than stopping at school placement, we focused on working while studying — running English résumé courses and company visits.

The relationships we built in Australia grew into PR support for Japanese companies and local governments. Having people here, going in person and bringing back results: the approach is the same for students and for businesses.

Women Can Fly is now SkyLink

What began as career support for women now reaches students, companies and local governments. We want to connect Japan and Australia for everyone, whatever their gender or role — so we have changed our name to SkyLink. Our track record and team carry over unchanged.

Founder

Founder & Director / 會澤 貴美代Kimiyo Aizawa

Born in Kobe. After working for a company in Japan, she moved to Australia in 2018 and founded the business in Sydney in 2019.

Driven by a wish to help people take a first step they cannot quite take alone, she works on career support for international students and Australian PR for Japanese companies and local governments.
