About us

Broadening options for a brighter future.

Connecting people, ideas and things of value across borders. That is the work we began in Sydney in 2019.

Mission.

Our mission

Expanding possibilities beyond borders.
Connecting people with the world.

Through education, careers, corporate PR, events and international business, SkyLink connects people with people, businesses with businesses, and communities with the world.

Our role is not to decide anyone's future for them. By creating new information, experiences, encounters and chances to take on a challenge, we help each person broaden their own possibilities. We want those connections to create new value, and individual growth to carry on into the growth of businesses and whole communities.

Women Can Fly is now SkyLink

Although we were called Women Can Fly, our mission was never about supporting women exclusively. We want everyone, whatever their gender or role, to be free to choose their own path — and we wanted our name to say so. Our origami bird carries on in new colours, and so do our track record and team.

Vision.

The future we're working towards

A world where everyone has the freedom and opportunity to shape their own future.

We believe no one's potential should be limited by their nationality, gender, age, culture, upbringing or past experience.

Success and happiness look different for everyone. We are working towards a society where each person can choose their path by their own values and feel free to take on new possibilities.

Core Values

Our 7 core values

The shared standards behind how we make decisions, take action and approach our work.

  1. 01

    Can-Do AttitudeFind a way to make it happen.

    We never decide from the start that something can't be done. Difficulties and challenges are exactly when we look hardest for how to make things happen. Instead of listing reasons why not, we look for ways it can work, and we value taking the initiative and taking on challenges ourselves.

  2. 02

    Integrity & TrustAct with integrity. Build trust.

    We act with integrity at all times. We keep our promises, share accurate information and take responsibility for what we say and do. Trust isn't built by one big achievement, but by the small things we do every day.

  3. 03

    Create Shared ValueMake it work for everyone involved.

    We don't chase profit or success for ourselves alone. We think about the value we can create for our clients, partners, team and society. Rather than relationships where only one side gains, we build ones where everyone involved grows together and shares value over the long term.

  4. 04

    Respect & InclusionRespect differences. Widen possibilities.

    We respect differences in nationality, gender, age, culture, experience and values. Rather than pushing our own views onto others, we recognise each person's individuality and strengths, and we create the environment and opportunities for everyone to take on challenges as themselves and realise their potential.

  5. 05

    Never Stop Learning & Pursue ExcellenceKeep learning. Pursue excellence.

    We never settle for the status quo, and we keep taking in new knowledge, experiences and perspectives. Not knowing something is nothing to be ashamed of; not trying to find out is the real problem.

    What matters is moving forward every day, even a little. Learning one more thing than yesterday, raising the quality of our work a little more, taking one more step forward. Through small improvements and challenges, we keep pursuing higher quality and greater value.

    We don't fear change. We keep rethinking our own ideas and methods and making them better.

    Better today than yesterday, and better tomorrow than today.

  6. 06

    Turn Ideas into Real ImpactFrom ideas to results that matter.

    We are never satisfied with ideas and plans alone. However good an idea is, it creates no value until it is put into action. We understand the purpose, act on it and see it through to the end, and we care that the result helps someone and leads to real value and change.

  7. 07

    Make It Meaningful & FunDo work that means something.

    We don't see our work as a list of tasks. We ask what it is for and who it will help, and we find meaning in it. We value curiosity and creativity, enjoy the process of taking on challenges, and aim for work that has a positive influence on the people around us.

Our Culture

Our culture

It's about uplifting the entire community.

Synergy for everyone we work with: new value and possibilities that would never have existed if we hadn't met.

We value the chemistry that happens when people, businesses and cultures connect. When different strengths, experiences and values come together, things no one could achieve alone become possible.

SkyLink creates those encounters and connections, and an environment where everyone involved can grow together.

Attitude is Everything.

Everything starts with how we approach things.

Skills can be learned. Experience can be gained. But attitude determines how we grow and contribute.

We care about the attitude we bring to our work and to people.

Integrity, responsibility, a willingness to learn and respect for others. Looking for ways to make things happen, acting on our own initiative and owning what we say and do.

We believe each person's positive attitude builds trust, helps the team grow and creates greater value.

Philosophy.

We don't define success for people.

I don't want to tell people what they should become.
I want to help create a world where they have the freedom to decide for themselves.

Kimiyo Aizawa, Founder

Although my organisation is called Women Can Fly, our mission has never been about supporting women exclusively. At the heart of what we do is a simple belief: everyone deserves equal opportunities to reach their full potential, regardless of their gender, background, or circumstances.

We focus on women because gender inequality still exists, particularly in areas such as the gender pay gap and unpaid care work. But my vision is much broader. I believe everyone should have the freedom to define what a fulfilling life means to them.

If a man wants to be a stay-at-home father while his wife pursues her career, that should be a perfectly natural choice. Equally, if a woman wants to focus on her family rather than her career, that choice should be respected too. For me, gender equality is not about encouraging everyone to follow the same path. It's about removing the barriers and expectations that limit people's choices.

I want to create a world where people can take ownership of their own lives and make choices they genuinely believe in. And for as long as I live, I hope to contribute, even in a small way, to making that world a reality.

History.

Our history

People who want to study or work abroad. Companies and regions that want Australians to know what they offer. What they share is not knowing how to take the first step.

We started with career support for international students. Rather than stopping at school placement, we focused on working while studying — running English résumé courses and company visits.

The relationships we built in Australia grew into PR support for Japanese companies and local governments. Having people here, going in person and bringing back results: the approach is the same for students and for businesses.
