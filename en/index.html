Why SkyLink

Being here makes the difference.

Translating a message into English is not enough to reach Australians. Who to tell, in what order, and where — we work that out locally, and then we do the work.

  • On the ground

    Based in Sydney. We show up.

    We stand at the booth, visit the shops and schools, and hear what visitors say first-hand. We don't stop at translation and documents; we report back what actually happened.

  • Network

    Direct ties to Australian government and business.

    Our founder, Kimiyo Aizawa, is a board member of the Australia Japan Society of NSW — an organisation with more than 55 years of history, where around 70% of members are Australian.

  • People & Business

    We know both sides: people and companies.

    Since 2019 we have supported international students' careers while running PR for Japanese companies and local governments. Our advice draws on both everyday life in Australia and the local market.
