Why SkyLink

Being here makes the difference.

Translating a message into English is not enough to reach people overseas. Who to tell, in what order, where and how — we work that out on the ground, with an understanding of the local context, and then we do the work.

  • On the ground

    Based in Australia. We show up.

    We visit shops and schools, present at trade show booths, and hear what visitors say first-hand. We don't stop at translation and documents; we share what we tested on the ground, as it is.

  • Network

    Direct ties to Australian government and business.

    Our founder, Kimiyo Aizawa, is a board member of the Australia Japan Society of NSW — an organisation with more than 55 years of history, where around 70% of members are Australian.

  • People & Business

    We know both sides: people and companies.

    Since 2019 we have supported students who study abroad seriously, with their careers in mind, while running PR events for Japanese companies and local governments. Our advice and support draw on both the reality of life in Australia and the local market.
