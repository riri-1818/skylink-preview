What we do

How we can help

Tailor-made

Built one project at a time, around your goal.

Exhibiting at a trade meeting, hosting a delegation, launching a store. A different goal means a different plan. From a one-day event to support that runs for years, hand over just the parts you need.

  • Exhibitions

    Trade show and trade meeting support

    JAPAN EXPO, SAKE FESTIVAL, Snow Travel Expo, MATSURI JAPAN, trade meetings with travel companies and more. We help choose the right event, run the booth, handle English on the day and record every conversation.

  • Events

    Event planning and operations

    Store opening receptions, product PR events, tastings, networking events and corporate seminars. We plan the event, arrange the venue, staging and MC, secure sponsors and run it on the day.

  • Media & PR

    Local media and social media PR

    Inviting Australian media and pitching for coverage, plus outreach through influencers, social media and Japanese and local communities — reworked into words and visuals that land with people here.

  • Visits & hosting

    Hosting delegations and local arrangements

    From coordination before you arrive to airport transfers, vehicles, dinners and venue bookings, accompanying and interpreting on visits, and managing movement on the day. We also arrange meetings with government contacts.

  • Sales & matching

    Sales calls, meetings and introductions

    Choosing who to visit among travel companies, distributors, retailers, restaurants and bars, setting appointments, accompanying you and interpreting. We also introduce local suppliers and partners.

  • Research & strategy

    Market research and strategy

    Interviews with shops and travel companies, and conversations with event visitors. Figures are checked against official sources and reported with citations, with recommended next steps.
