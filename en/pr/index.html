Bring what Japan does best to Australia — in person.

What we do

How we can help

  • Exhibitions

    Trade show and event support

    Choosing the right event — JAPAN EXPO, SAKE FESTIVAL, Snow Travel Expo and others — running the booth, handling English on the day and recording visitor reactions.

  • Local PR

    PR and marketing in Australia

    Outreach to Australian media, influencers and Japanese and local communities, reworked into words and visuals that land with people here.

  • Visits & meetings

    Site visits and business meetings

    Selecting who to visit, arranging appointments, accompanying you and interpreting — travel companies, distributors, retailers and government contacts.

  • Research

    Market research and interviews

    Interviews with shops and travel companies, and conversations with event visitors. Figures are checked against official sources and reported with citations.

  • Events

    Event planning and operations

    Product PR events, tastings, networking events and corporate seminars, including sponsorship and on-the-day operations.

  • Translation

    Translation and English copy

    Websites, company profiles, presentations and product descriptions — written for Australian readers, not translated word for word.

Who we work with

Who comes to us

  • Local governments, tourism associations and DMOs seeking more Australian visitors
  • Food, drink and confectionery makers testing the Australian market
  • Craft and lifestyle brands wanting real reactions overseas
  • PR and advertising agencies needing research or local support in Australia
  • Organisations planning an event or visit in Sydney
  • Anyone not yet sure where to start
