Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Information we collect

We collect the name, company or organisation, email address, phone number and message you enter in our enquiry and consultation forms.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry, to propose and provide our services, and to contact you when necessary.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share personal information with third parties without your consent, except where required by law. Where study arrangements require us to pass details to a school or accommodation provider, we confirm with you first.

Security

We manage personal information appropriately to prevent leaks or loss.

Access, correction and deletion

If you ask to see, correct or delete your information, we will confirm your identity and respond promptly. Please use the contact form.

Applicable law

We handle personal information with regard to the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information.
