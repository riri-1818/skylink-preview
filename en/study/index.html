Study in Australia, with work in mind.

Programs

Three ways to come

  • Up to 3 months | Visitor visa (ETA etc.)

    Short-term study

    For a long holiday or a gap between jobs. Up to three months of study is possible on a visitor visa, so there is less paperwork and it is easy to start.

    • Language school + homestay
    • Junior programs in summer and spring
    • English plus travel or a short qualification
  • 6 months+ | Student visa

    Long-term study

    For those who want to build their English properly, or plan vocational or further study. You can also work within set hours while you study.

    • English plus specialist skills
    • Work experience while studying
    • Travel during course breaks
  • Working Holiday visa

    Working holiday

    Combine study, work and travel at your own pace. Many people attend a language school for just the first few months.

    • Flexible planning
    • English résumé and job search support
    • One-on-one support for first-timers

Visa conditions change. We check the latest on the Australian Department of Home Affairs website and go through it with you in your consultation.

Steps

From consultation to departure

  1. Set your goal and timeframe

    English, a qualification, work experience or a new culture. Knowing why you are going makes choosing a school easier.

  2. Free consultation

    An Australia-based counsellor works through your English level, budget and timeframe — and the career you want to build through study abroad.

  3. Choose a school

    Compare curriculum, location and cost. It helps to keep more than one option.

  4. Apply

    Submit your application and pay the enrolment fee. We help with the documents.

  5. Enrolment and visa

    After your offer arrives: tuition, accommodation, insurance and your visa application.

  6. Before you leave

    Flights, packing, currency and phone setup. Finish your English résumé now too.

  7. Travel

    If you have an airport pickup, we will send the details in advance.

  8. Life and work in Australia

    We are here after you arrive. Our LINE account shares job listings and local tips every week.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I study in Australia as a beginner in English?

Yes. Language schools test your level on entry and have beginner classes. Preparation before you leave makes a real difference, so in your consultation we suggest schools for your level and how to study until departure.

How much does it cost?

It varies widely with length, city, school and accommodation. The main costs are tuition, accommodation, flights, insurance, visa fees and living expenses. We give you an estimate based on your plans in your consultation.

Student visa or Working Holiday visa?

A student visa suits those who want to study seriously or plan vocational or further study. A Working Holiday visa suits those who put work experience and flexibility first (age and other conditions apply). For up to three months, short-term study on a visitor visa is also an option.

Can I work while studying?

Student visas have a limit on working hours. Working Holiday visas allow work, with conditions such as how long you can work for one employer. You cannot work on a visitor visa. Conditions change, so we check the latest with you.

Will my English improve on a short stay?

With less time, a clear goal matters more — getting used to speaking, or trying out a city before a longer stay. We also share ways to spend more of your stay using English.
