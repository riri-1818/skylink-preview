Our work

Projects we have delivered, mainly in Sydney, since 2019. Includes work carried out under our former name, Women Can Fly (WCF).

Japan–Australia event2025

Co-hosted Spring in Sydney (Sakura no Kai)

Together with the Australia Japan Society of NSW, where our founder is a board member, we co-hosted a Japan–Australia networking event in Sydney. It brought together business and government figures from both countries; we handled everything from securing sponsors to how the night looked and felt.

Partner
Australia Japan Society of NSW (co-host)
Our role
PR / sponsorship / branding
Scale & outcome
Around 370 guests from business and government
Destination marketing2022–

Trade show and business meeting support for HAKUBA VALLEY (Nagano)

As part of HAKUBA VALLEY's PR for the Australian market, we supported its presence at business meetings and trade shows for Australian travel companies — presenting at the booth, building contacts and coordinating site visits. Beyond translating the appeal of the destination, we work out who to tell and in what order.

Partner
HAKUBA VALLEY, Nagano Prefecture
Our role
Trade show support / on-site presentation / site visit coordination

Destination marketing2026

Sydney visit, PR and marketing for Horokanai, Hokkaido

We support the Australia-facing visit, PR and marketing programme of Horokanai, one of Japan's snowiest towns. We coordinated meetings and exchanges in Sydney, visited the town to meet its mayor, and are discussing tour product development and next year's activity in the Australian market. We plan the next step, not just a single event.

Partner
Horokanai Town, Hokkaido
Our role
Visit coordination / PR / marketing support
When
Sydney visit, February 2026
