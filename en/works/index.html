Career event2025

Unleash Your Potential: an International Women's Day career networking event with student societies from three universities

On 5 March 2025, to mark International Women's Day, we held the event at PwC Australia's Barangaroo office. In its second year, the partnership grew from one university to student societies from three, and the students themselves ran the promotion, the programme on the night and the sponsor introductions. Three professionals from Canva, Atlassian and Hort Innovation shared their career journeys and talked about bias related to gender and cultural background, and the challenges international students face when job hunting. A lucky draw and networking time were made possible by local restaurant and business sponsors. In 2026 the student societies went on to organise the event themselves across universities.

Partner
Student societies AJC (UNSW), JAM (Macquarie University) and JCN (University of Sydney) (co-hosts) / Venue: PwC Australia
Our role
Overall planning and operations support / sponsorship
Scale & outcome
Around 80 students and young professionals
When
5 March 2025
