This program is perfect for students who:

✨ Want to study abroad for the first time

✨ Want to challenge themselves away from home

✨ Want to experience a new culture and make international friends

Homestay life, English classes, and daily activities all become part of a life-changing journey.

Many students arrive feeling nervous and uncertain. Being away from their parents for the first time, they experience homesickness, challenges, frustrations, and personal growth.

Just a few weeks later, they return home more confident, independent, and ready for their next adventure.

Looking after someone else's child is a huge responsibility, but seeing that growth is one of the greatest joys of this work.

Every year, I am reminded of my own mother, who encouraged me to study abroad when I was in Year 10.

That experience changed my life.

This summer, give your child the opportunity to discover a new world.

🌏✈️ We would love to support their first step towards an international future.
