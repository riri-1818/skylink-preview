Canva Create in Sydney, Australia

Productivity × Creativity: Shaping the Future of Work and Creation

In 2025, We were honoured to be invited once again as an Canva Empower Canvassador to Canva Create in Sydney 2025, a global event hosted by Canva.

Bringing together creators, educators, and entrepreneurs from around the world, Canva Create is a unique space where we can experience the present and future of Canva’s vision — a world where anyone can create.

Theme of Canva Create 2025

Productivity × Creativity

One message was consistently shared throughout Canva Create 2025: Productivity and creativity are not a trade-off.

More than 40 new features announced this year were all developed from real user feedback.

“I wish Canva could do this.”That wish has now become reality.

Key New Canva Features (2025 Highlights)

Magic Write

Instantly generate copy and written content with AI

Magic Media

Automatically create images and videos from text prompts

Canva Sheets

Bulk-create multiple designs directly from spreadsheets

Canva Code

Build widgets with no coding required

AI Photo Editor

One-click background removal and replacement

All-in-one Workspace

Integrate design, text, and data into a single file

These updates are not just about saving time.

They are designed to unlock individual creativity and expand human potential.

The Value of Real Connections in a Digital World

In an era where we can connect online with ease,the value of real-world events and communities becomes even more meaningful.

Canva Create in Sydney was a space filled with warmth —where technology met people, and creativity met genuine intention.

Gratitude to Canva — and the Future of Women Can Fly (WCF)

At Women Can Fly (WCF), we support people who pursue study abroad and global careers by helping them:

  • Express their ideas

  • In their own words

  • To the world

Canva plays a powerful role in lowering the barriers to self-expression, regardless of language ability or design experience.

At WCF, we have seen many students and professionals regain confidence and move forward through creative tools like Canva.

To the Canva event team — thank you once again for such an inspiring experience.　And to the WCF team and community who continue to support our mission, my heartfelt gratitude.

Women Can Fly will continue to support those who choose to take on the world through creativity.
