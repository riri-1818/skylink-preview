Event report: SYDNEY Career Networking Event 2025 “Unleash Your Potential” – 80 attendees

On Wednesday 5 March 2025, to mark International Women’s Day, Women Can Fly.Co (WCF) held “Unleash Your Potential – Accelerate Action for Career Growth” at PwC Australia’s Barangaroo office.

In its second year, the event grew from a partnership with one university to student societies from three. Around 80 students and young professionals came together to explore new career possibilities by meeting professionals working at leading companies.

Growing to three universities

The Australia-Japan Career Development Society (AJC) at UNSW, the Japanese Association Macquarie (JAM) at Macquarie University and the Japan Career Network (JCN) at the University of Sydney planned and ran the event together. WCF supported the whole project, while the students themselves led the promotion, ran the programme on the night and introduced the sponsors.

Sharing real career experience

In the panel discussion, our speakers shared their own experiences of work and careers.

  • Yurie Takagi (Technical Program Manager, Canva)
  • Ethan Yew (Product Manager, Atlassian)
  • Mimi Doan (International Trade Manager, Hort Innovation)

They talked about how their careers took shape, what they did at university, bias related to gender and cultural background, and the challenges international students face when looking for work. With plenty of questions from the students, it was a valuable chance to hear directly from people working in the field.

Learning, connecting and having fun

As in the previous year, PwC Australia generously provided the venue. In 2025, restaurants and businesses also supported us with drinks, coffee and meal vouchers so students could relax, network and enjoy a lucky draw.

Why Women Can Fly keeps running this event

WCF’s everyday work is supporting study and careers in Australia. But for us, the value of studying abroad is not only about travelling overseas and going to school. It is about meeting people, taking on challenges, building connections with the community and expanding what you can do. That is the real value of studying abroad.

This event is not run for profit. We keep holding it because we believe the experiences and connections students gain outside the classroom are what support their future lives and careers.

Students creating the next opportunity

Another important goal is for students to be able to create these opportunities themselves, working with universities and companies even without WCF leading. One university in the first year, three in the second, and in 2026 the student societies went on to build cross-university partnerships and organise the event themselves.

Not one organisation providing opportunities forever, but participants creating new opportunities for the next person. That is what WCF has been working towards.

Thank you

Our heartfelt thanks to our speakers; to PwC Australia for the venue; to Ichoume, Toby’s Estate, Jazushi, Gaku Robata Grill and all our sponsors and supporters; and to the student members of AJC, JAM and JCN and everyone who joined us.

Towards a society where everyone can choose a life that is true to themselves, whatever their nationality, gender or cultural background.
Unleash Your Potential.
